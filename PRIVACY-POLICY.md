# Privacy Policy — Video Catch

_Last updated: 24 July 2026_

Video Catch ("the extension") is designed to work entirely on your own device.
This policy explains, in plain terms, what the extension does and does not do
with your information.

## The short version

Video Catch does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information. There are no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no servers
operated by us. Everything the extension does happens locally in your browser.

## What the extension accesses, and why

To find and save videos you choose to download, the extension needs to look at
the pages and network requests in your browser. Specifically:

- **The page you are on** — to detect `<video>` elements and the page title so a
  saved file can be named sensibly.
- **Network requests in your browser** — to recognise video streams (such as
  `.m3u8`, `.mpd`, or `.mp4` requests) so they can be offered as downloads.
- **Your browser downloads** — to save the files you ask it to save.
- **Local browser storage** — to remember your settings (for example your
  preferred quality, save folder, and whether the on-screen "Catch" button is
  shown) and your download history list.

All of this information is used only on your device, only to perform the action
you requested, and only for as long as needed to perform it. None of it is sent
anywhere.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does not collect or store your browsing history.
- It does not send any data to us or to any third party.
- It does not contain analytics, advertising, or tracking code.
- It does not require an account, email address, or any sign-in.
- It does not read, store, or transmit passwords, cookies, or form data for any
  purpose other than the browser's own authenticated requests needed to fetch a
  video you chose to download.

## On-device processing

Converting and merging video (for example combining separate audio and video
tracks, extracting audio, trimming clips, or making GIFs) is performed locally
using a bundled copy of FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly. No media file is ever
uploaded to any server for processing.

## Permissions

The browser permissions the extension requests (access to downloads, web
requests, notifications, local storage, offscreen documents, declarative net
request rules, and website access) exist solely to provide the video-saving
features described above. They are not used to collect information about you.

## Data retention

Your settings and download history are stored in your browser's local storage
and remain on your device until you clear them (via the extension, or by
removing the extension). We never receive this data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same
location as this document and the "Last updated" date will change.
